## Describe your idea for a data analysis final project
My project will look at community water fluoridation and oral health across the United States. 
In 2025, Utah became the first state to ban flouride in public dinking water, and Florida followed soon after. 
That makes this a good to ask whether states where more people drink fluoridated water actually have better dental
health. 

Main question: Do states with a higher percentage of people on fluridated community water have lower rates of tooth
loss in adults?

Other questions: 
- Does the relationship still hold after accounting for income and how often people go to the dentist?
- Does it differ by region of the country?
- Where did Utah fall compared to other states before the ban? 

I expect states with more fluoridation to have less adult tooth loss, but I also expect income and dental visits to explain part of that difference. 

Planned analysis:
- Scatter plots of fluoridation vs tooth loss, colored by region
- A linear model: pct_teeth_lost 
pct_fluoridation + pct_dental_visit + median_income 
- New skill: a U.S. map showing fluoridation and tooth loss by state
## Also, Describe the sort of data you will use, potential sources, and what the data might look like
State level public health data. Each row will be one state (50 states plus DC so about 51 rows), could 
be repeated across a few survey years. 

Potential sources:
- CDC water fluoridation statistics/ water fluoridation reporting system (percent of each state's population on fluoridated water)
- CDC oral health data portal, which pulls from the BRFSS survey (tooth loss and dental visits by state)
- U.S> census bureau american community survey (median household income by state)
- News coverage of the 2025 Utah and Florida fluoride bans for background
All public and I will join them together by state (and year).

Example of what data might look like:
-state: state name 
-region: Northeast, midwest, south, or west 
pct_fluoridated: percent of population on community water that receives fluoridated water #0-100
- median_income: median household income in dollars 
pct_dental_visit: percent of adults who visited a dentist in the past year
- pct_teeth_lost: percent of adults 65+ who have lost 6 or more teeth
